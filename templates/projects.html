Owl: Hidden Camera Detection

Owl is a HarmonyOS app that helps users check indoor spaces for hidden cameras. It scans nearby wireless signals, flags suspicious activity, and uses colored rings and audio cues to help narrow down where to look.

The app runs offline on select Huawei devices with HarmonyOS. Nearby electronics and environmental interference can affect results. Users should inspect flagged areas in person.
